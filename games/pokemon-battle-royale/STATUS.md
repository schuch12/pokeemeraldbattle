## Technical notes
- Hub map: `data/maps/BattleRoyaleTown`, layout generated by `games/pokemon-battle-royale/tools/make_hub.py` (a crop of BattleFrontier_OutsideEast).
- The hub reuses MAPSEC_BATTLE_FRONTIER (renamed) so met-location data stays vanilla-compatible.
- New game: `src/new_game.c` (warp, starter, flags), intro shortcut in `src/main_menu.c`.
- Center / Mart / shop reuse the BattleFrontier_PokemonCenter_1F, BattleFrontier_Mart and BattleFrontier_Lounge5 interiors; their exits now lead to the hub.
- Mart stock is in `data/maps/BattleFrontier_Mart/scripts.inc`. Shop script is at the end of `data/maps/BattleFrontier_Lounge5/scripts.inc`; its menu text is in `src/data/script_menu.h` (prices appear in both places).
- Berry prices raised from ₽20 to ₽100 (`src/data/items.h`) so Oran Berries don't undercut Potions.
- Blackout point is HEAL_LOCATION_BATTLE_FRONTIER_OUTSIDE_EAST, moved to the hub's Center.
- Tower: `src/battle_royale.c` holds the tier table (opponent identities, species pool, levels, prize money). Lobby and battle room are new maps `BattleRoyale_TowerLobby` / `BattleRoyale_TowerBattleRoom` reusing the vanilla Tower layouts.
- Tower battles are normal trainer battles (EXP and items allowed) started by `BattleRoyale_StartTowerBattle` in `src/battle_setup.c`; the enemy team and prize money are overridden in `battle_main.c` / `battle_script_commands.c` while `gBattleRoyaleTowerBattle` is set. Losing never whites out.
- Run state is in vars 0x40F7–0x40FA (previously unused), see `include/battle_royale.h`.
- Soft level cap: `BattleRoyale_GetLevelCap` (cap of the first uncleared tier: 15, then 25); applied in `Cmd_getexp` in `battle_script_commands.c`, EXP cut to 10% at or above the cap.
- Wild Pokémon for the hub grass are the `MAP_BATTLE_ROYALE_TOWN` entry in `src/data/wild_encounters.json` (lv 4–12). Blacking out no longer halves money (`DoWhiteOut`).
- Save and quit: the between-battles menu (NEXT BATTLE / SAVE AND QUIT / RETIRE) sends the player to the lobby with the run kept in vars 0x40F7–0x40FA; `VAR_BR_PAUSED` (0x40FB) makes the lobby offer the normal save once. Talking to the attendant resumes or abandons the run. The player is never saved inside the battle room, so a reload cannot soft-lock.
- Save format unchanged from vanilla (Flash 128K, same block layout; only previously unused vars are used).
