## Technical notes
- Hub map: `data/maps/BattleRoyaleTown`, layout generated by `games/pokemon-battle-royale/tools/make_hub.py` (a crop of BattleFrontier_OutsideEast).
- The hub reuses MAPSEC_BATTLE_FRONTIER (renamed) so met-location data stays vanilla-compatible.
- New game: `src/new_game.c` (warp, starter, flags), intro shortcut in `src/main_menu.c`.
- Center / Mart / shop reuse the BattleFrontier_PokemonCenter_1F, BattleFrontier_Mart and BattleFrontier_Lounge5 interiors; their exits now lead to the hub.
- Mart stock is in `data/maps/BattleFrontier_Mart/scripts.inc`. Shop script is at the end of `data/maps/BattleFrontier_Lounge5/scripts.inc`; its menu text is in `src/data/script_menu.h` (prices appear in both places).
- Berry prices raised from ₽20 to ₽100 (`src/data/items.h`) so Oran Berries don't undercut Potions.
- Blackout point is HEAL_LOCATION_BATTLE_FRONTIER_OUTSIDE_EAST, moved to the hub's Center.
- Save format unchanged from vanilla so far.
